Added: `calendar` and the `/calendar` skill, the dates a game's marketing plans around: Steam's seasonal sales, Next Fest editions, themed fests and the Black Friday hub read from Valve's Upcoming Steam Events at run time, the most-joined itch.io jams, Epic's giveaways and discount windows, and comparable games' prices, lowest price seen and deepest discount from LootRadar's public API (`-Comparable` or `release.comparables`). With `release.date` it says which seasonal sales the game can join, whether `release.nextFest` is an edition Valve lists (or names the last one before release with its demo deadline), and writes `calendar-results/calendar.ics`. Pages are cached 6 hours in `.godot/hyperdot-calendar`; `-Offline` reads only the cache; one source failing is a warning, all failing is `calendar-unavailable`. plan.json's `release` gains `launchDiscount`, `discounts` and `comparables`, and `plan` and `status` now check the discounts against Steam's discounting rules (30 days after release, 30 days between discounts with seasonal sales exempt, 10 to 40% and 7 to 14 days for a launch discount). `help <name>` shows both entries when a command and its skill share the name.
