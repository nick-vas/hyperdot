Added: `test -FixedFps` (workflow input `fixed-fps`) also runs the Godot that gdUnit4 starts at fixed game time, not only `smoke`, so gdUnit4 tests that wait for physics ticks no longer wait for the wall clock; on the C# example its tests went from 36 s to 13 s and `test` from 59 s to 36 s. A rate other than the game's physics tick rate is a warning; `-FixedFps` on another command or below 0 is `invalid-arguments`. Off by default: audio, the network and .NET timers still follow the wall clock.
