Fixed: a batch of defects in the kit's libraries. Failure text with control characters (BEL, ESC, NUL) no longer makes the JUnit report unreadable. `credits` now fails with `engine-notices-failed` when Godot cannot read the engine's notices, instead of writing a file without Godot's licence. `-Frames` below 1 and, for `playtest`, a `-HotspotCell` of 0 or less are refused as `invalid-arguments`, and `export -Preset` is checked before Godot's templates are downloaded. `status` skips history lines that are not runs, reads the history once, and the history is cut to the newest 2,000 lines. A failed `bench -Sweep` run no longer reports an earlier sweep's numbers. The `data` runner no longer crashes on a table without `fields` or on an invalid `pattern`. `plan` and `plan -Do add` work on a plan with no `tasks`, and the plan keeps the game's name as written. `sfx` gives different seeds different files and makes a sound named twice once. A tiny `playtest` cell is reported as `invalid-playtest-config` instead of crashing. `soak` refuses a `-Seconds` too short to measure memory growth when `-MaxGrowthMbPerHour` is set.
