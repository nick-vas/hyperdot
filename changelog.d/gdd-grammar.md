Changed: the GDD feature grammar is a rule the kit enforces, not advice: each feature has exactly one `Priority:` line (must, should, could, won't) and exactly one `Fun:` line holding only kinds of fun, comma-separated, or `none`, which only a could or won't feature may have; feature ids are unique. `doctor` fails on every other form with the line and the one fix to make (an explanation on the `Fun:` line gives "Write `Fun: challenge` and put the rest on a line of its own"), and `status` lists the same problems in `gdd.problems` and its warnings. Fixed: `Priority: won't` was read as `won`, so won't features were warned about as missing plan tasks. The benchmark games' GDDs gain a `Fun:` line on every feature; their menus-and-saves and languages features become `could` with `Fun: none`, so token-benchmark runs before and after this change are not strictly comparable.
