Added: `brief` and the `/new-game` skill, the structured start of a new game. The user describes the game in their own words (`brief -Do start -TextFile`); `brief -Json` then lists, four at a time and in a fixed order, the questions from `ci/godot/templates/brief-questions.json` that are still open (basics: title, genre from `genres-uw.json`, 2D or 3D, camera, platforms, controls, GDScript or C#, players and network, core loop, session, kinds of fun, pillars, failure, saves; scope: jam, small or full, its length or hours a week, comparable games, out of scope; look and sound: art style, resolution, colours, sound), each with at most four options. An answer is taken from the description only with `-Quote`, the sentence that states it, found in it word for word; every other answer comes from asking the question as written, and anything outside its options is refused (`invalid-answer`). Rules across answers (C# with a Web export, touch with no touch platform, no kind of fun) block the draft with their fix. `brief -Do gdd` writes a draft `docs/GDD.md` that passes `doctor`'s feature grammar and, when there is none, `docs/STYLE.md`, and names the next steps (the Godot project, `plan -Do init` with the jam or full profile, `/game`). It needs no Godot project. New error codes `brief-missing`, `brief-exists`, `invalid-answer`, `brief-incomplete` and `gdd-exists`.
