Fixed: `plan`'s `slice.export`, `jam.export` and `launch.gold` gates promised `export -Run` but checked only that an export preset exists, so they held even after a failed export. A new check, `export-run`, holds when every preset this machine can launch passed its newest `export -Run` and was launched; `export` records `run`, `presets` and `launched` in its history line, trimming keeps the newest `export -Run` line, `nextActions` gives each open gate's `detail`, and once a later phase has begun a newer failure is a warning instead of reopening the gate. Plans made before keep `preset`, and `plan` warns about those three gates until they use `export-run` (#288).
