Added: `telemetry`, one read-only view over `.godot/hyperdot-history.jsonl`: per command the runs, pass rate and mean, median and p95 duration of the newest `-Last` runs (50) with the trend of the newer half against the older, and for `test` the slowest runners, the tests that fail most often, the flaky ones and the recurring error keys. A history too thin for a number leaves it `null` and says `insufficient`; a missing file gives zero runs, a damaged line is skipped and counted. `test` now also records each runner's seconds (`runnerSeconds`) in its history line.
