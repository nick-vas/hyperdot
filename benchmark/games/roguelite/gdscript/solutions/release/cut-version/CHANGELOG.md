# Changelog

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-24

- The version shown on the menu and in the project settings is 1.1.0.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-23

- A descent through four floors of a crypt, each generated from the run's seed: rooms joined by corridors, every one reachable.
- Shades, cinder bats and tallow brutes that wander until they see the wick, then hunt it; walls block their sight and their steps.
- Floors sealed until their last enemy is snuffed; the stairs relight one hp.
- Embers banked across runs and spent on vigor at the shrine; the run in progress kept for Continue.
- Menu, pause, settings (volume, English, French, German), credits; saves from version 0.x (embers and the deepest floor) still load.
