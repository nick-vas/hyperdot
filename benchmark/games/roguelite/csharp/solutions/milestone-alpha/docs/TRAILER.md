# Trailer script

1. 0-5 s: a candle in a dark crypt, shades closing in from the edge of its light: a top-down roguelite.
2. Hook: the last shade snuffed, and the sealed stairs glowing open.
3. Moments: a swarm of cinder bats pouring down a corridor, a tallow brute winding up, the shrine buying the first vigor.
4. Every mechanic with its feedback: a blow sounds, a hurt wick flashes, shakes and sounds, a snuffed enemy leaves a ring
   of light and a crack.
