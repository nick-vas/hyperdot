# Duskhive

**Pitch:** an idle game of a moth hive at dusk: gather glow, raise wick moths, glow snails and star looms whose
prices grow with each one bought, and greet the dawn to trade a whole night for embers that make every night brighter.

**Pillars:** a choice worth making every minute; numbers that grow past what a phone can count, always readable;
time away that counts, and never more than it should.

## Features

### F1 Producers
Priority: must
Done when:
- five producers from data, each price the last one's times its growth; buy 1, 10 or as many as the glow pays for

### F2 Upgrades
Priority: must
Done when:
- upgrades that multiply a producer, Gather or everything, offered once their producer is owned often enough

### F3 The dawn
Priority: must
Done when:
- a reset for embers (the square root of the run's glow in millions), each adding 10% to all production

### F4 Time away
Priority: must
Done when:
- the time since the save is paid on start, up to 8 hours; a clock set back pays nothing and never pays twice

### F5 Saves
Priority: must
