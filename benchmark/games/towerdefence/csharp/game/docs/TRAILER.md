# Trailer script

1. 0-5 s: a line of pests marching across a grid of grass while thorns fire at them: a tower defence.
2. Hook: a single thorn planted in the pests' way, and the whole column turning at once onto the longer way round.
3. Moments: an acorn mortar's splash taking out a cluster, the Briar Maze turned into a long maze, the slug queen falling.
4. Every mechanic with its feedback: a planted tower rings and chimes, a falling pest pops, a pest reaching the store
   flashes and shakes the screen.
