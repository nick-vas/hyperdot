# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-24

- A tower defence in a hedge garden: pests walk the shortest way to the seed store, and every tower planted reroutes them.
- Three towers with two upgrades each; selling gives back most of what a tower cost.
- Three maps of 4, 5 and 6 waves, the last ending with the slug queen.
- Menu, map select, pause, speed, settings (volume, English, French, German), credits.
- The best stars of each map saved; saves from version 0.x (a list of maps cleared) still load.
