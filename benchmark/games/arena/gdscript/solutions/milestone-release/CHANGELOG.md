# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-25

- A top-down arena for four: move, aim, fire sparks and dash round the pillars; five hits put a glimmer out, the last
  one standing wins the round, and three rounds win the match.
- Two courts, the Cinder Pit and the Hollow Ring, played in turn.
- Play against bots, host a match others join over the network, or join one; bots fill every empty slot, and players
  may come and go in the middle of a match.
- An authoritative server with client prediction and reconciliation, and a dedicated server mode (--server).
- Menu, pause, settings (volume, English, French, German), credits; settings and your record saved, and settings from
  version 0.x (volume in decibels) still load.
