# Trailer script

1. 0-5 s: four coloured glimmers circling the pillars of a dark pit, sparks crossing: an arena shooter.
2. Hook: the last two standing on match point, one dashing out from behind the core pillar for the final spark.
3. Moments: a player joining mid-match and taking a bot's place, a three-way crossfire, the match won 3 to 2.
4. Every mechanic with its feedback: a spark fires with a beam and a zap, a hit glimmer flashes white and sounds, a
   glimmer knocked out leaves a ring of light, a shake and a burst.
