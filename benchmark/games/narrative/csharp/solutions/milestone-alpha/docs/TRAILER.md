# Trailer script

1. 0-5 s: a ferry in fog, a line of text typing out above two choices: a narrative game.
2. Hook: the stranger's face pale in the lantern light, and the choice to steady yourself or hurry.
3. Moments: the lantern glowing as she trusts you, the drowned tower, the river filling with lanterns.
4. Every mechanic with its feedback: a choice clicks, trust makes the lantern glow and chime, an ending flashes and rings.
