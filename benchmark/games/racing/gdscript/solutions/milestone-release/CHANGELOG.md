# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-24

- Three tracks, from Ashfall Oval to Caldera Hairpins, against three rivals who drive the racing line at their own pace.
- Checkpoints that must be crossed in order, lap times to the thousandth, and the ghost of the best lap to race against.
- Keys rebound in Settings and kept, with the gamepad working throughout.
- Menu, pause, settings (volume, keys, English, French, German), credits.
- Best laps and their ghosts are saved; saves from version 0.x (best laps in seconds) still load.
