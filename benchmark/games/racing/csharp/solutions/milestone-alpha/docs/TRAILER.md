# Trailer script

1. 0-5 s: four little cars burst off the grid onto a cinder oval, seen from above: a racer.
2. Hook: the player dives past two rivals on the brakes into a hairpin, the ghost of the best lap a car's length ahead.
3. Moments: the esses taken flat out, the hairpins of the caldera, the last lap's line crossed a tenth up on the ghost.
4. Every mechanic with its feedback: the start flashes and beeps, a pass pops the place with a chime, a lap pops the
   lap counter with a rising tone.
